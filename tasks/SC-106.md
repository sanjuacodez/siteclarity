## Purpose
The deterministic half of AI Readiness that concerns document structure. **No LLM.** This is
what makes the product useful with no key and no cost.

## Scope — in
- **Structured data**: JSON-LD parsed and validated; type appropriateness; FAQPage/HowTo/Product/Article detection; malformed JSON-LD reported as a finding rather than crashing.
- **Heading hierarchy**: exactly one H1; no skipped levels; headings descriptive rather than decorative ("Overview" vs "How SiteClarity handles redirects").
- **Question-shaped headings**: interrogative phrasing detection.
- **Answer proximity**: substantive text present immediately after each heading, before the next subheading.
- **Extractable formats**: presence and proportion of lists, tables, definition lists versus undifferentiated prose.
- **Passage length distribution**: flag passages too long to extract cleanly and stubs too short to answer anything.
- **Machine access**: meta robots, canonical correctness, `noai`/`noimageai` signals, and the `robots.txt` outcome from SC-103.

Each check emits `Finding[]` per SC-105, with evidence passage IDs attached.

## Scope — out
No language or tone analysis (SC-107). No model calls (SC-109). No prioritisation or
dedupe (SC-111).

## Files owned
`src/static/structure/**`

## Acceptance criteria
- Each check has fixtures for the passing case, the failing case, and at least one ambiguous case that must yield `cannot_assess` rather than a guess.
- Malformed JSON-LD produces a finding, never an exception.
- Every emitted finding carries at least one valid passage ID.
- Runs correctly with the provider disabled entirely.
- Within CPU budget on the largest fixture (SC-114).

## Validation
```
npm test -- static/structure
```
